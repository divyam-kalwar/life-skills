# Prevention of Sexual Harassment

## Question 1
**What kinds of behaviour cause sexual harassment?**

Sexual harassment includes any unwanted behaviour of a sexual nature. It can happen through words, actions, gestures or messages. Some examples are:

1. Unwanted physical contact, touching or repeated advances.
2. Asking for sexual favours or making unwanted sexual requests.
3. Making sexual jokes, comments or remarks about someone's appearance.
4. Showing or sharing inappropriate pictures, videos or other sexual content.
5. Making unwanted gestures, staring or sending inappropriate messages.
6. Threatening someone or offering benefits at work in exchange for sexual favours.
7. Creating an uncomfortable, intimidating or hostile work environment through such behaviour.

## Question 2
**What would you do in case you face or witness any incident or repeated incidents of such behaviour?**

1. If I feel safe, I would clearly tell the person that their behaviour is inappropriate and must stop.
2. I would note down the date, time, place and details of the incident, and keep any relevant messages, emails or other evidence.
3. If I experience the behaviour, I would report it to the company's HR team or Internal Complaints Committee (ICC) by following the organisation's POSH policy.
4. If I witness such behaviour, I would support the affected person, listen without judging and encourage them to report it. I would also report it through the proper workplace channel.
5. I would avoid spreading the matter among colleagues and respect the privacy of everyone involved.
6. If there is an immediate threat to someone's safety, I would help them move to a safe place and contact the appropriate authorities.
