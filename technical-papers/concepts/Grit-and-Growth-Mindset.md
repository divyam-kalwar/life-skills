# Grit and Growth Mindset

## Question 1
**Paraphrase (summarize) the video in a few (1 or 2) lines. Use your own words.**

Grit means having passion for a long-term goal and continuing to work towards it even when things get difficult. Success is not just about talent; patience, effort and consistency also matter.

## Question 2
**Paraphrase (summarize) the video in a few (1 or 2) lines in your own words.**

A growth mindset is the belief that our skills and intelligence can improve through learning, practice and effort. Instead of thinking that we are either good or bad at something, we should believe that we can get better with time.

## Question 3
**What is the Internal Locus of Control? What is the key point in the video?**

Internal Locus of Control means believing that our actions, choices and efforts have an important role in shaping our results. The key point in the video is to focus on what we can control, take responsibility for our actions and recognise that our effort can make a difference.

## Question 4
**What are the key points mentioned by speaker to build growth mindset (explanation not needed).**

1. Believe in your ability to figure things out.
2. Question your assumptions.
3. Create your own curriculum for growth.
4. Honour the struggle.

## Question 5
**What are your ideas to take action and build Growth Mindset?**

1. I will break difficult topics into smaller parts and work on them step by step.
2. I will focus on understanding concepts instead of just memorising or copying code.
3. When my code does not work, I will try to understand the mistake instead of getting frustrated or giving up.
4. I will ask questions, explore different solutions and take feedback positively.
5. I will practise regularly and track my progress instead of comparing myself with others.
6. I will take responsibility for my learning and make time to improve the areas where I am weak.
7. I will treat challenging tasks as opportunities to learn something new.
