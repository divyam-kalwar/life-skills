# Learning Process

## Question 1
**What is the Feynman Technique? Explain in 1 line.**

The Feynman Technique is a way of learning by explaining a concept in simple words, as if you were teaching it to a child.

## Question 3
**What are active and diffused modes of thinking?**

- **Active Mode:** A focused way of thinking where we concentrate on a task and work through it step by step.
- **Diffuse Mode:** A relaxed way of thinking that helps us connect ideas and understand things from a broader perspective.

## Question 4
**According to the video, what are the steps to take when approaching a new topic? Only mention the points.**

1. Break the skill into smaller parts.
2. Learn enough to identify and correct your mistakes.
3. Remove distractions and anything that makes it difficult to practise.
4. Practise for at least 20 hours.

**What are some of the actions you can take going forward to improve your learning process?**

1. I will avoid distractions while studying and use the Feynman Technique to check whether I really understand a topic.
2. I will make use of both focused and diffuse modes of thinking instead of forcing myself to work continuously.
