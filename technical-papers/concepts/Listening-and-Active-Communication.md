# Listening and Active Communication

## 1. Active Listening

#### Question 1
**What are the steps/strategies to do Active Listening? (Minimum 6 points)**

1. Give the speaker your full attention and avoid distractions.
2. Let the other person finish speaking instead of interrupting.
3. Focus on what the speaker is saying rather than thinking about your reply.
4. Show that you are listening through eye contact and suitable body language.
5. Ask questions if something is not clear.
6. Use short responses to encourage the speaker to continue.
7. Repeat or explain the main points in your own words to check your understanding.
8. Take notes during important conversations so you do not miss any details.

## 2. Reflective Listening

#### Question 2
**According to Fisher's model, what are the key points of Reflective Listening? (Write in your own words, use simple English)**

1. Give the conversation your full attention and remove distractions.
2. Try to understand the speaker's point of view without judging them. You do not have to agree with everything they say.
3. Notice their feelings through their words, tone of voice and body language.
4. Reflect their feelings and message so they know you have understood them.
5. Summarise the main points using the speaker's words as much as possible.
6. Respond to the point being discussed instead of changing the subject.
7. Give the speaker time to think and speak. Do not fill every silence with unnecessary words.
8. Continue listening and reflecting as the conversation moves forward. If needed, switch roles and let the other person speak.

## 3. Reflection

#### Question 3
**What are the obstacles in your listening process?**

Sometimes I get distracted by my own thoughts while someone is speaking. I may start thinking about how I should respond instead of focusing on what the person is saying. I also find it difficult to stay focused during long conversations, especially when the topic is unfamiliar. At times, I assume that I have understood the point before the person has finished explaining it.

#### Question 4
**What can you do to improve your listening?**

1. Keep my phone away and avoid distractions during conversations.
2. Listen until the other person finishes instead of interrupting.
3. Focus on understanding the message rather than preparing my reply.
4. Ask questions whenever I am unsure about something.
5. Take notes during meetings and technical discussions.
6. Summarise important points to confirm that I have understood them correctly.

## 4. Types of Communication

#### Question 5
**When do you switch to Passive communication style in your day to day life?**

I sometimes become passive when I am speaking with someone senior or when I want to avoid an argument. In such situations, I may not share my opinion even when I have something important to say. I usually do this because I do not want to upset the other person or make the situation uncomfortable.

#### Question 6
**When do you switch into Aggressive communication styles in your day to day life?**

When I feel frustrated or when things do not go as expected, I may respond in a harsh tone without thinking properly. Sometimes, I focus more on proving my point than understanding the other person's side. I need to work on staying calm and expressing my disagreement respectfully.

#### Question 7
**When do you switch into Passive Aggressive (sarcasm/gossiping/taunts/silent treatment and others) communication styles in your day to day life?**

When I am upset with someone but do not feel comfortable talking about it directly, I may avoid the conversation or use sarcasm to show that I am unhappy. Sometimes, staying silent feels easier than explaining what is bothering me. However, this can create misunderstandings instead of solving the issue.

#### Question 8
**How can you make your communication assertive? You can watch and analyse the videos, then think what would be a few steps you can apply in your own life? (Watch the videos first before answering this question.)**

1. Express my thoughts and opinions clearly without being rude.
2. Learn to say no politely when I am not comfortable with something.
3. Listen to the other person's point of view and respect their opinion, even when I disagree.
4. Talk about problems directly instead of using sarcasm or avoiding the conversation.
5. Stay calm during disagreements and focus on finding a solution.
6. Use statements like "I feel" or "I think" to explain my point without blaming others.
7. Ask questions if I do not understand something instead of making assumptions.
